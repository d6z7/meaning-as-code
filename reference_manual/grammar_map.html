MAC · three grammars

Is folding a grammar? Is a ruling?

Folding is one by the estate's own naming — FOLD_GRAMMAR.md, which QUERY_GRAMMAR.md calls "the orthogonal axis". A ruling is not called one anywhere, and it has the same three parts as one: a closed vocabulary of words, a composition that says what each word contributes, and an outcome. So all three are drawn in the same three lanes below — and what differs is the middle lane.
